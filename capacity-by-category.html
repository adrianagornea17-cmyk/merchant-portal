Data notes

  • Q1 2026 is excluded — no worklogs exist in Jira for Q1-tagged tickets at all (spot-checked directly); worklog tracking for this team appears to have started around Q2 2026, and there's no Q1-2026 sprint either.
  • Scope: only Epics with fixVersion "[Q2/Q3/Q4 2026] Verifone Central" and Stories/Defects with a matching Sprint quarter tag, filtered to tickets with at least one worklog from the 7-person tracked roster (Anand BS, Sruthi Nakul Mysore, Senthil Chidambaram, Akshat Aggarwal, Tanya Saxena, Ashish Verma, Sumit Ashish). Q2 = 39 of ~56 pool tickets, Q3 = 96 of ~113 (grew from 94 tickets last refresh — one new epic, two new stories picked up worklogs), Q4 = 41 of ~160 (sprint "Ready for dev (Q4-26, Aug-Oct)"; grew from 37 — two new stories, plus two epics that previously only had children with worklogs now have direct worklogs of their own) — tickets nobody on the roster logged time against are excluded, not counted as zero.
  • Every quarter here counts ALL roster hours ever logged on a qualifying ticket, regardless of the worklog's own date — not just hours logged within that quarter's calendar window. This is a deliberate, consistent choice across Q2/Q3/Q4.
  • Q4 2026 is a quarter in progress (runs through Oct 31) — its 1,566.0h total will keep growing as the quarter continues, unlike Q2/Q3 which are closed periods. Q2 and Q3 also grew since the last refresh (+58.0h and +60.0h respectively) from backdated/newly-logged worklogs on already-tracked tickets — this is expected and not a data error; nothing here is ever truly "final" until a quarter is well behind us.
  • "Not Set" means the field was genuinely empty on that ticket, or (for Capitalization/Program Category, which only live on Epics) the story had no parent Epic to inherit from. A parent Epic living in a different Jira project (e.g. GGW, SGC, ENT) is NOT automatically "Not Set" — those cross-project epics were queried directly and do carry real values (see ET-738, ET-2908, ET-3196 in the exception table below, all resolved this way).
  • Q2's "BAU Work" total is dominated by two tickets: ET-1098 ("Meeting & Collaboration Hours," ~277h) and ET-1527 (the "Sprint Ceremonies" epic itself, ~250h) — both catch-all-style tickets worth a sanity check. Q4's "BAU Work" (572.5h) and "Global-UI" (1,254.5h) totals are similarly dominated by ET-2944 "Verifone Central MFE & VDS Workshop."
  • "Technical Debt" is a bucket that only appears in Q4 — distinct from "Technical Improvement" (both are separate allowed values on the Software Capitalization field). Only one ticket, ET-3196, whose parent epic (ENT-42389) lives in a different Jira project (ENT, not ET) but does have its own resolvable value.
  • Hours normalized from Jira's worklog shorthand using the standard 8h/day, 40h/week convention.
  • Last refreshed 2026-09-26.
