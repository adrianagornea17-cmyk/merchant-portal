Data notes

  • Q1 2026 is excluded — no worklogs exist in Jira for Q1-tagged tickets at all (spot-checked directly); worklog tracking for this team appears to have started around Q2 2026, and there's no Q1-2026 sprint either.
  • Scope: only Epics with fixVersion "[Q2/Q3 2026] Verifone Central" and Stories/Defects with a matching Sprint quarter tag, filtered to tickets with at least one worklog from the 7-person tracked roster (Anand BS, Sruthi Nakul Mysore, Senthil Chidambaram, Akshat Aggarwal, Tanya Saxena, Ashish Verma, Sumit Ashish). Q2 = 39 of ~56 pool tickets, Q3 = 94 of ~110 — tickets nobody on the roster logged time against are excluded, not counted as zero.
  • "Not Set" means the field was genuinely empty on that ticket, or (for Capitalization) the story had no parent Epic to inherit from, or its parent Epic lives in a different Jira project.
  • Q2's "BAU Work" total is dominated by one ticket (ET-1098, "Meeting & Collaboration Hours," 271.9h across three people).
  • Hours normalized from Jira's worklog shorthand using the standard 8h/day, 40h/week convention.
